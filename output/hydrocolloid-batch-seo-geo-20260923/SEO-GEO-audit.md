# Food Ingredients SEO/GEO Audit — 2026-09-23

## Scope and decision

This batch reviewed nine Foodchem reference pages against Bespring's actual English catalogue. The reference pages were used to identify useful buyer questions and specification fields; their wording and unsupported claims were not copied.

Four real Bespring equivalents already had differentiated, technically useful copy. They were therefore not rewritten. Each received one missing Schema.org `Product` entity so search engines and answer engines can connect the product identity, alternate names, key identifiers, image and enquiry action to the visible page.

## Comparison findings

The reference pages make commercial facts such as package, MOQ, container load and a compact specification easy to scan. That is useful for qualified enquiries. Their templates also mix broad marketing language with application lists, and some pages cross into unrelated end uses. Bespring's four existing pages are stronger where purchasing decisions depend on grade boundaries, methods and application conditions.

The useful next step for these pages is evidence-led specification data rather than more general prose:

- **Pectin:** HM/LM/LMA type, degree of esterification, degree of amidation where relevant, calcium reactivity, standardization system, set temperature and gel test method.
- **Cocoa powder:** natural or alkalized process, fat on stated basis, pH method, colour system and target, fineness, dispersibility and microbiological limits.
- **Gum arabic / acacia gum:** botanical source, raw or spray-dried form, emulsification or encapsulation performance method, viscosity method and microbiological limits.
- **Carrageenan:** refined carrageenan (INS 407) versus processed Eucheuma seaweed (INS 407a), kappa/iota/lambda system, carrier salts or standardizers, gel-strength method, ionic conditions and process conditions.

## Inputs required before creating the five missing pages

New product pages should be created only after Bespring confirms that the product is actually supplied and provides a current specification or COA. Minimum inputs are:

- **Titanium dioxide:** intended markets and permitted use, exact identity and purity, particle-size method, surface treatment if any, application scope, packaging and destination-specific regulatory documents.
- **Rosemary extract:** active basis (for example carnosic acid/carnosol or rosmarinic acid), assay method, carrier and extraction solvent, solubility, residual-solvent limits, antioxidant application and stability data.
- **Triacetin:** identity and CAS 102-76-1, assay, acidity, water, relative density, intended food use, packaging and compliance documents.
- **Agar:** botanical source, gel strength with method and concentration, moisture, ash, particle size, microbiological limits and application trials.
- **Gelatin:** animal source and species, Bloom strength and method, viscosity and method, particle size, microbiology, traceability, Halal/Kosher status where claimed and application trials.

## Change boundary

No keyword-volume or Google Trends numbers were invented. No supplier, certification, stock, regulatory, proprietary-grade or performance claim was added without evidence already present in the repository. Canonicals, hreflang, visible copy, titles and descriptions were left unchanged because the four pages were already mature and differentiated.
