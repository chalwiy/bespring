SALP Product Overview & Specifications

Sodium Aluminum Phosphate (SALP) is a slow-acting leavening acid widely used in double-acting baking powder systems. It delivers controlled and reproducible gas release during baking, supporting consistent volume, texture, and product quality in industrial bakery applications.

Product Identity

Grade: Food Grade (FCC) CAS No.: 7785-88-8

Typical Applications

  • ✔️ Double-acting baking powder formulations
  • ✔️ Cake pre-mixes and frozen dough systems
  • ✔️ Biscuits, cookies, crackers, and premixes
