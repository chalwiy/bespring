Products

Bespring Chemical provides trusted ingredients and raw materials across multiple industries, including food, feed, water treatment, homecare & industrial cleaning, and mineral processing. With decades of expertise and a global supply network, we deliver solutions that meet diverse application needs with consistent quality.

Food Ingredients →

We provide high-quality ingredients for the food industry, supporting innovation and consistency across a wide range of categories, including bakery, beverages, dairy, meat & seafood, prepared foods, and snacks.

Animal Nutrition →

We deliver comprehensive solutions for premix, compound, and mineral feed producers, offering amino acids, trace elements, vitamins, and other essential nutrients to support animal health and performance.

Homecare & Industrial Cleaning →

We supply a complete portfolio of raw materials for detergents and cleaning products, including surfactants, solvents, oleochemicals, and specialty chemicals to enhance cleaning efficiency and product performance.
