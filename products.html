Procurement guidance

What to include in a useful RFQ

A precise inquiry helps us match product, grade, source, documents and logistics without unnecessary back-and-forth.

For regulated or safety-sensitive materials, your technical and regulatory teams should review the current specification, SDS and market requirements before approval.

See procurement support services

01

Product identity

Full chemical name, abbreviation, grade, CAS number and reference standard.

02

Critical limits

Assay, moisture, pH, particle size, viscosity, contaminants or other acceptance criteria.

03

Required documents

Specification, SDS, COA format and any applicable certificates for qualification.

04

Delivery basis

Quantity, packing, pallets, destination port, Incoterm and requested shipment window.

Buyer documentation

Documents to align before approval

Document availability and scope are product- and source-specific. Request current documents for the exact supply offered.

SPEC

Specification / TDS

Identity, grade, reference limits and technical characteristics.

SDS

Safety Data Sheet

Classification, handling, storage and transport information.

COA

Certificate of Analysis

Representative or batch test results against the agreed specification.

CERT

Applicable Certificates

Current quality, product or site documents relevant to the supply.
