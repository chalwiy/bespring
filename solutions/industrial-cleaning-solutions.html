Start with the cleaning task

Build the Formula Around the Required Function

Cleaning performance usually comes from a coordinated system rather than one raw material. These are the core functions to balance.

Wetting & Detergency

Reduce surface tension, lift soils and keep removed material dispersed in the cleaning bath.

Solvent Action

Support grease dissolution, drying behavior, clarity or moisture management where appropriate.

Alkalinity & Building

Adjust pH, support soil removal and help manage water conditions in detergent systems.

Acid Descaling

Dissolve mineral scale and support controlled cleaning of acid-compatible surfaces.

Oxidation & Bleaching

Address oxidizable stains and color bodies through oxygen- or chlorine-based systems.

Tell Us What the Product Must Do

A useful recommendation depends on the full cleaning context. Include these details when you contact our team.

  • Application: laundry, hard surface, degreasing, descaling or plant cleaning
  • Soil: oil, particulate matter, protein, mineral scale or colored stain
  • Surface: metal, glass, ceramic, polymer, textile or mixed materials
  • Process: manual, spray, immersion, foam or circulation cleaning
  • Formulation: liquid or powder, pH range, foam profile and target concentration
  • Supply: required grade, annual volume, packing and destination country
Selection principle

Frequently Asked Questions

Concise answers to common sourcing and formulation questions from cleaning-product manufacturers.

Which chemicals are commonly used in homecare and industrial cleaning formulations?

Common functional families include surfactants for wetting and detergency, solvents for oily soils, inorganic builders and alkalis for pH and cleaning power, acids for descaling, and oxidizing agents for bleaching. The right combination depends on the substrate, soil and process.

How should we choose between an acid cleaner and an alkaline cleaner?

Acid systems are generally considered for mineral scale and inorganic deposits, while alkaline systems are often used for oils, fats and heavy organic soils. Surface compatibility, concentration, temperature and handling requirements must be evaluated before use.

Can Bespring supply several cleaning ingredients in one order?

Bespring supports multi-product sourcing subject to product compatibility, transport classification, order volume and destination requirements. Share the complete requirement so packaging and shipping options can be reviewed together.

What documents are available for industrial cleaning chemicals?

Typical documentation may include product specifications, certificates of analysis, technical data sheets and safety data sheets. Availability and format depend on the product and requested grade.

Are bleaching or antimicrobial claims automatically allowed for a finished cleaner?

No. Finished-product claims, registrations, permitted active substances and labeling requirements vary by market and application. Buyers should validate the finished formulation and comply with the regulations of the destination market.
