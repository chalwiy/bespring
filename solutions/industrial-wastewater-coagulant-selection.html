Search questions answered

Questions specific to industrial wastewater coagulant selection for solids separation

Can a wastewater coagulant be selected from COD alone?

No. Industry source, particulate versus soluble COD, pH, alkalinity, oils, surfactants, metals and the separation equipment all affect selection.

PAC, ferric chloride or an organic coagulant—which should be tested?

Their charge, pH demand, sludge and contaminant interactions differ. Use actual wastewater and the intended separator; no universal ranking applies.

Can this page provide a final formula or dosage?

No. It defines a technically relevant shortlist and evidence plan. Final use level and approval require the exact grade, actual process data, qualified technical review and applicable local rules.
