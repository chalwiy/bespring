Search questions answered

Questions specific to phosphates for meat and poultry processing

Which ingredients are commonly screened for processed meat and poultry?

Phosphate systems, lactates, antioxidants, hydrocolloids and functional proteins may address different parts of the process. The correct system depends on the meat, formula, equipment, market and measured failure.

What is the difference between STPP, TSPP and TKPP in processed meat?

They differ in alkalinity, solubility and sequestration behavior. The useful choice depends on brine temperature, ionic strength, protein system, process time and local phosphate limits, so equal-dose substitution is not a reliable approval method.

Can this page provide a final formula or dosage?

No. It defines a technically relevant shortlist and evidence plan. Final use level and approval require the exact grade, actual process data, qualified technical review and applicable local rules.
