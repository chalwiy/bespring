MCP and DCP should be compared on the phosphorus basis accepted by the poultry nutritionist, together with calcium contribution, impurities, physical quality and delivered cost.

MCP commonly offers a higher phosphorus-to-calcium ratio than DCP, while DCP contributes more calcium relative to phosphorus. Exact values vary by product and analytical basis; the supplier's guarantee must be entered into the complete broiler, layer or breeder matrix rather than copied from a generic table.

Phytase assumptions, basal-ingredient phosphorus, bird phase and local terminology for available or digestible phosphorus can change the economic result. A lower price per tonne may cost more per unit of approved usable phosphorus.

Qualification path: verify identity and legal classification, compare guarantees and COA variation, review fluorine and undesirable-substance limits, assess particle distribution and flow, then recalculate the complete diet before mill and bird-performance evaluation.

Application-specific approval

Qualify the grade in the diet and the mill

Give the formulation and feed-mill teams separate approval checks for the same material.

01

Identify the exact source

Record whether the material is MCP or DCP, the declared feed grade, the production site and the supplier's specification and recent lot COAs.

02

Recalculate the diet

Ask the nutritionist to enter each candidate on the same analytical and digestibility basis. Recheck phosphorus, calcium and the phytase credit before comparing cost.

03

Check handling and mixing

Compare particle distribution and fines with the current grade. Look for bridging, dust or segregation during the site's approved receiving and mixing checks.

04

Approve a documented material

Set acceptance limits, test methods, lot records and change-notification requirements before approving the grade or source.

Performance evaluation

What to record for purchasing approval

Keep numerical specifications, nutrition assumptions and plant observations separate in the acceptance record.

Analysis and contaminants

Compare phosphorus, calcium, moisture and named impurities using the same reporting basis and specified methods. Record samples and lot numbers.

Record the usable-phosphorus value, its method and the bird phase and diet assumptions under which it was accepted.

Physical and economic fit

Confirm mill handling and the approved cost comparison, including the effect of calcium contribution, freight, handling and fit with the plant.

Feed-mill questions

Questions about MCP and DCP for poultry feed

Should MCP and DCP be compared by price per tonne?

No. Compare cost per unit of phosphorus accepted in the nutrition matrix, then account for calcium contribution, freight, handling and quality variation.

Does total phosphorus equal available or digestible phosphorus?

No. The usable value depends on the source, analytical and nutritional basis, bird phase, basal diet and phytase matrix. Use values approved by the responsible nutritionist.

Which supplier documents matter for feed phosphate?

Request the exact specification, recent COAs, analytical methods, feed-market declarations, traceability, SDS where applicable, and limits for fluorine and destination-market undesirable substances.
