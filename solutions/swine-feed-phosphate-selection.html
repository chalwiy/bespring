Search questions answered

Questions specific to nursery pig feed acidifiers amino acids and phosphate sources

How do MCP and DCP fit into swine feed?

They supply calcium and phosphorus, but practical value depends on digestible phosphorus, calcium balance, source consistency, fluorine, contaminants and delivered cost—not total phosphorus alone.

When are fumaric and citric acid screened in pig feed?

They are among the organic acids evaluated in nursery and other swine diets, but response varies with dose, basal ingredients and age. A qualified nutritionist must set the trial and legal use conditions.

Can this page provide a final formula or dosage?

No. It defines a technically relevant shortlist and evidence plan. Final use level and approval require the exact grade, actual process data, qualified technical review and applicable local rules.
