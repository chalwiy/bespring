Frequently asked questions

Before you send an inquiry

How to route a purchasing or technical request.

What information should I include in a quotation request?

Please include the product name or specification, grade, estimated quantity, destination country or port, required packaging and any certification or documentation needs.

Where can I find the product or grade name?

Start with the product catalog. If you are still comparing grades, describe the application, required standard and performance target in your inquiry.

How do I specify a document or sample request?

Identify the product, grade, destination and any required tests or certification scope. For a sample, include the application and the quantity needed for your evaluation.
