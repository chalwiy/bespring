Procurement guidance

Define reagent grade and process context

Mining chemical requests should state the exact reagent, active content or purity, physical form, critical impurities, consumption estimate, packing and destination logistics.

Collector series, frother composition, lime reactivity, flocculant charge and molecular weight, and reagent concentration can materially affect qualification. Use plant or laboratory test results to define the required specification.

Important: Many mining reagents are corrosive, toxic, oxidizing, flammable or dangerous to the environment. Selection, testing, storage and use require qualified metallurgical and HSE oversight.

01

Ore & process context

Identify commodity, ore type and process stage so commercial discussions use the correct reagent family and grade.

02

Reagent specification

State active content, purity, solution strength, particle size or polymer characteristics relevant to the material.

03

Site logistics

Provide monthly demand, packing, storage constraints, mine location, destination port and preferred Incoterm.

04

HSE documentation

Request the current SDS, transport classification and handling information for the exact concentration and source.

Buyer documentation

Documents to align before approval

Availability and scope depend on the exact product and source offered. Request current, applicable documents during qualification.

SPEC

Specification / TDS

Product identity, grade, reference limits and technical characteristics for commercial review.

SDS

Safety Data Sheet

Classification, handling, storage, transport and workplace-control information for the offered material.

COA

Certificate of Analysis

Representative or batch-specific test results compared with the mutually agreed specification.

CERT

Applicable Certificates

Current quality, product or site documents where relevant to the offered supply and destination.
