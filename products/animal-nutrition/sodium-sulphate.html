What is Feed Grade Sodium Sulphate for animal nutrition?

Feed-grade sodium sulphate is a sodium and sulfur source used in suitable animal-nutrition formulations; hydration form, sodium and sulfur contribution, contaminants and legal status require confirmation.

Suitability depends on the exact product, target species, life stage, complete diet, intended technological or nutritional function and destination-market authorization. This page is procurement guidance, not an inclusion-rate recommendation.

Feed Grade Sodium Sulphate feed application guide

These are screening areas, not universal authorizations, feeding instructions, dosage recommendations or guaranteed outcomes.

Defined mineral supplementation

Use sodium sulfate only within a qualified formulation addressing a measured requirement. Account for both sodium and sulfur.

Mineral premixes

Review compatibility and particle matching with the other components. Check the analytical composition of the finished mix.

Ration mineral review

Assess drinking water and basal-feed contributions before selecting the grade. Keep the source specification separate from inclusion decisions.

Feed-mill qualification

Validate addition accuracy, moisture protection and lot traceability. Require current composition and impurity evidence.

Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Feed Grade Sodium Sulphate

Anhydrous or hydrated salt

Identify the hydration form and assay basis. Compare sodium, sulfur and water contributions using the offered grade's actual composition.

Total sulfur

Have the nutritionist assess sulfur from the full diet and drinking water. Source approval must account for the target species and complete ration.

Purity and physical grade

Agree relevant contaminant limits, moisture and particle requirements. Verify flow and uniform distribution in the intended blend.

Feed-use documentation

Confirm the source, intended nutritional role and destination status. A technical-grade identity does not establish suitability for animal feed.
