Procurement guidance

Specify active content and physical form

Cleaning-chemical quotations should identify active matter or concentration, physical form, key impurity limits, packing and dangerous-goods requirements.

Commercial names can cover different concentrations and grades. LABSA, SLES, hydrogen peroxide, hypochlorite-related materials and caustic alkalis require particularly clear concentration and transport information.

Important: Acids, alkalis, oxidizers and chlorine-releasing products can present serious handling and compatibility hazards. Review the current SDS and applicable transport classification before purchase and use.

01

Chemical identity

Provide the exact chemical name, CAS number where relevant and the commercial concentration or active content.

02

Formulation parameters

State required pH, color, viscosity, solids, salt content or other critical limits used in incoming quality control.

03

Packing & compatibility

Confirm drum, IBC, bag or other packing and check material compatibility for the concentration supplied.

04

Transport status

Provide destination and shipment mode so classification, dangerous-goods documents and packing can be reviewed.

Buyer documentation

Documents to align before approval

Availability and scope depend on the exact product and source offered. Request current, applicable documents during qualification.

SPEC

Specification / TDS

Product identity, grade, reference limits and technical characteristics for commercial review.

SDS

Safety Data Sheet

Classification, handling, storage, transport and workplace-control information for the offered material.

COA

Certificate of Analysis

Representative or batch-specific test results compared with the mutually agreed specification.

CERT

Applicable Certificates

Current quality, product or site documents where relevant to the offered supply and destination.
