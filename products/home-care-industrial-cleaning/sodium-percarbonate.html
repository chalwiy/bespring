Sodium Percarbonate FAQ

What is Sodium Percarbonate used for in cleaning formulations?

Sodium percarbonate is a solid peroxygen source used in laundry and cleaning powders; available oxygen, coating, granulation, moisture protection and formulation compatibility drive performance. Suitability must be established in the complete formulation and intended use.

Which Sodium Percarbonate identity should buyers specify?

CAS 15630-89-4; Sodium carbonate–hydrogen peroxide adduct. Product families, solutions, hydrates and mixtures require the exact commercial form to be stated.

Why is there no public specification?

Bespring Chemical does not publish specifications for this industrial cleaning ingredient online. Contact sales for the current source-specific specification or TDS, methods and representative COA.

What documents should industrial buyers request?

What should the RFQ include?
