Procurement guidance

Compare nutrient analysis, not abbreviation alone

Fertilizer salt procurement should confirm the chemical grade, declared nutrient content, solubility, moisture, insolubles, impurity limits and particle-size or form requirements.

MAP, DAP and potassium phosphates can be supplied to different specifications. An abbreviation does not establish nutrient analysis, suitability for a particular fertilizer system or destination-market registration.

Important: Compatibility, crop use, nutrient rate and local fertilizer registration must be assessed separately. This page does not provide agronomic recommendations.

01

Chemical grade

State fertilizer, technical or another required grade and provide the reference specification.

02

Nutrient declaration

Confirm the phosphorus, potassium or nitrogen basis used for commercial comparison and labeling.

03

Purity & solubility

Define moisture, water insolubles, chloride, heavy metals and particle-size limits where relevant.

04

Commercial details

Provide quantity, bag size, palletization, destination, registration documents and shipment timing.

Buyer documentation

Documents to align before approval

Availability and scope depend on the exact product and source offered. Request current, applicable documents during qualification.

SPEC

Specification / TDS

Product identity, grade, reference limits and technical characteristics for commercial review.

SDS

Safety Data Sheet

Classification, handling, storage, transport and workplace-control information for the offered material.

COA

Certificate of Analysis

Representative or batch-specific test results compared with the mutually agreed specification.

CERT

Applicable Certificates

Current quality, product or site documents where relevant to the offered supply and destination.
