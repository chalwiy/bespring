Application screening

Quaternary Ammonium Compounds (QACs) water-treatment application guide

These are technical screening areas—not universal approvals, operating directions, efficacy claims or guaranteed outcomes.

Industrial cooling-water biocide formulations

Assess the complete cooling-water program using its water chemistry, residence time and operating conditions. Track microbial control alongside material compatibility, treatment-chemical interactions and blowdown requirements.

Process-water microbial-control programs

Define the microbial target, process-water demand and contact conditions for the finished program. Include contact materials, downstream product requirements and residual or discharge limits in validation.

Oilfield and closed-loop water formulations

Assess the finished formulation in representative brine or circulating water. Include compatibility with other additives, relevant microbial targets and the system's discharge or reuse requirements.

Registered non-potable disinfectant products

Qualify the raw material against the finished formulation and its permitted use. The manufacturer must establish efficacy, compatibility and the authorization of the complete product.

Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Quaternary Ammonium Compounds (QACs)

Exact QAC active, CAS and homolog distribution

Identify each QAC active by its full name and CAS and request relevant homolog information. QAC describes a family of substances, not a single interchangeable biocide.

Active concentration, solvent and pH

State active concentration, solvent system and pH on the purchase specification. Compare the complete supplied formulation rather than only the nominal active percentage.

Anionic compatibility and water demand

Review compatibility with anionic ingredients and assess demand under the actual water conditions. Test the finished program rather than assuming raw-material activity transfers unchanged.

Biocidal registration, discharge and residue requirements

Verify the intended finished use, relevant biocidal authorization and discharge requirements. A raw-material grade is not a substitute for an approved treatment-product label.

Which Quaternary Ammonium Compounds (QACs) identity should buyers specify?

Can Quaternary Ammonium Compounds (QACs) be used for drinking-water treatment?
