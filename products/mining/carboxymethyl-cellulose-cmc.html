Flotation application

When to evaluate CMC for talc depression

Talc can report to the froth with the valuable minerals. In circuits where this affects concentrate quality, screen a depressant for its ability to reduce talc recovery while maintaining recovery of the valuable mineral.

Process water is part of that decision. A published talc microflotation study found that changes in water ionic strength affected CMC adsorption and talc recovery under the conditions tested. Use the water chemistry expected at your site when comparing candidates. Manono, Corin and Wiese, Minerals 2019.

CAS 9004-32-4 identifies sodium carboxymethyl cellulose. Use the grade specification and test results to distinguish materials with the same CAS number. NIH PubChem substance identity.

Test work

Plan a representative flotation comparison

  1. Set the baseline. Record the ore, target gangue, flotation stage and baseline grade and recovery.
  2. Document the water and reagent conditions. Include plant or recycle water, pH, solids content, collector, frother and conditioning sequence.
  3. Track the separation outcome. Compare concentrate grade, valuable-mineral recovery and mass pull together, then validate the preferred candidate at an appropriate scale before plant use.

Check mixing and transfer before approval

Assess how the proposed grade dissolves in your process water and whether it can be prepared and transferred with your existing equipment. Where slurry flow matters, measure it at the actual solids content. Record supplier solution viscosity and plant slurry behavior as separate measurements.

Documents to specify in your inquiry

  • Specification or technical data sheet (TDS). Confirm the grade, revision date, limits and test methods.
  • Safety data sheet (SDS). Match it to the material and physical form under review.
  • Certificate of analysis (COA). Use an example to check the reporting format. Verify delivered material against the COA for that batch.

Confirm storage, handling and site controls

Use the current SDS and your site procedures to determine handling, dust controls, personal protection and storage. Verify transport classification and packing for the supplied grade and destination. Evaluate downstream water and residue impacts under applicable site requirements before plant use.

Mining CMC purchasing questions

Is the CAS number enough to buy a flotation grade?

Specify sodium CMC and its CAS number, 9004-32-4, then identify the grade and acceptance criteria. Compare viscosity measured under the same test conditions, degree of substitution and physical form.

Can I use the same CMC dosage as another plant?

Establish the dosage with representative ore and process water. Record the reagent suite, conditioning sequence and test conditions. Compare concentrate grade, recovery and mass pull against the current baseline.

Which documents should I use for approval?

Use the grade specification or technical data sheet for the agreed limits and test methods. Match the safety data sheet to the supplied material and the certificate of analysis to the batch you receive.
