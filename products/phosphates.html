FAQ

Phosphate Product Qualification

How should buyers select a phosphate product?

Start with chemical identity, intended application, grade or standard, functional target, specification limits, test methods and destination-market requirements.

What is the difference between food, feed and technical grade phosphates?

Grades differ in intended use, specification and impurity limits, manufacturing controls, documentation and applicable regulatory requirements. A shared chemical name does not make grades interchangeable.

Can Bespring provide samples and quality documents?

Samples and documents are available according to the product and grade. Buyers should request the current specification or TDS, SDS, sample COA, packaging details and applicable supporting documents.
