Direct product answer

What is food grade Sodium Diacetate?

Food grade sodium diacetate is a molecular compound of sodium acetate and acetic acid, identified as INS 262(ii). In solution it provides acetate buffering and acetic-acid functionality, making grade composition and pH important procurement criteria.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, intended function and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern each transaction.
