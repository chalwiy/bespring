Product overview

What is food grade Sodium Diacetate?

Food grade sodium diacetate is a molecular compound of sodium acetate and acetic acid, identified as INS 262(ii). In solution it provides acetate buffering and acetic-acid functionality, making grade composition and pH important procurement criteria.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, intended function and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern each transaction.

Food grade Sodium Diacetate FAQ

What is food grade Sodium Diacetate used for?

Sodium Diacetate is used in selected food formulations for preservative, acidity regulator and flavoring ingredient functions. Suitability, legal use and dosage must be confirmed for the exact finished food and destination market.

What are the CAS and food additive numbers for Sodium Diacetate?

Sodium Diacetate is identified by CAS 126-96-5 and INS 262(ii). Confirm the exact supplied grade and current market rules.

What is the minimum order quantity for Sodium Diacetate?

The reference minimum order quantity is 500 kg. Sample, trial and commercial availability should be confirmed for the selected grade.

How is Sodium Diacetate packed?
