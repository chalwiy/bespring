Food Grade Distilled Monoglycerides (DMG)

Distilled monoglycerides are concentrated monoglyceride emulsifiers made from food-grade glycerides and fatty acids. DMG falls within the mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids family (INS 471), but commercial grades vary by monoglyceride level, fatty-acid profile, source and physical form.

Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Distilled Monoglycerides

Monoglyceride level

“DMG” is not a single composition; agree the test method and target in the private specification.

Fatty-acid profile

Source oils and degree of saturation affect melting and application behavior.

Physical form

Choose powder, beads, flakes or another available format for dosing and dispersion.

Origin claims

Confirm vegetable, animal, palm-related, allergen and certification requirements with evidence.
