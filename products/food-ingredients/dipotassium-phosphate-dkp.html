Direct product answer

What is food grade dipotassium phosphate?

Food grade dipotassium phosphate (DKP), also called dipotassium hydrogen phosphate or dibasic potassium phosphate, has formula K2HPO4, CAS number 7758-11-4 and INS number 340(ii).

The supplied grade has a reference assay of at least 98.0%. DKP is distinct from monopotassium phosphate and tripotassium phosphate; its alkalinity, pH range and functional behavior should be assessed in the actual formulation.

Functional roles

Why is DKP used in food processing?

DKP can perform more than one role. The final result depends on dosage, the complete mineral and protein system, process conditions and the destination market.

Function group 01

Buffering and acidity regulation

DKP may help establish or maintain pH in suitable beverage, dairy, fermentation and ingredient systems.

  • Buffering and acidity-regulator functions
  • Potassium and phosphate contribution
  • Process-pH consistency objectives
Function group 02

Water and emulsion management

The supplied application information identifies water retention and emulsification; JECFA also lists buffering, sequestration and yeast-food functions.

  • Moisture-retention support
  • Emulsification in selected formulations
  • Mineral-ion sequestration and culture support

Buyer questions

Food grade DKP FAQ

What is the difference between DKP and MKP?

DKP is K2HPO4 and INS 340(ii); MKP is KH2PO4 and INS 340(i). Their potassium-to-phosphate ratio, solution pH and formulation behavior differ.

What is food grade DKP used for?

It is evaluated as a buffer, acidity regulator, sequestrant, moisture-retention agent, emulsifier and culture nutrient in selected meat, cheese, beverage and fermentation systems.

What is the reference assay?

The supplied sheet states at least 98.0% K2HPO4. Confirm the current signed specification and batch COA for the offered supply.

What are the packing and minimum order?

The reference is 25 kg per bag, 22 metric tons per 20-foot container and a 500 kg minimum order. Confirm current liner, pallet and loading details.
