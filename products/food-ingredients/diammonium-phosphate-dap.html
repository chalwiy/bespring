Product overview

What is food grade Diammonium Phosphate?

Food grade diammonium phosphate (DAP), also called diammonium hydrogen phosphate, is a water-soluble phosphate used in suitable foods as an acidity regulator, dough conditioner, raising agent or yeast nutrient.

For procurement, “food grade” must be supported by a current source-specific specification, suitable manufacturing and quality documents, and a batch COA. Final use depends on the complete formulation, intended function and destination-market rules.

Buyer questions

Food grade Diammonium Phosphate FAQ

What is food grade Diammonium Phosphate used for?

Diammonium Phosphate is used in suitable food processes for phosphate, acidity regulator and yeast nutrient functions. Legal use, dosage and performance must be confirmed for the exact food and destination market.

What are the CAS and food additive numbers for Diammonium Phosphate?

Diammonium Phosphate is identified here by CAS 7783-28-0 and INS 342(ii). Confirm the identity and supplied form on the current specification.

What specification should a buyer request for Diammonium Phosphate?

Request the current signed specification, agreed test methods, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve every missing unit, comparator or particle-size requirement before approval.

How is food grade Diammonium Phosphate packed?

Confirm bag type, net weight and pallet requirement. Confirm food-contact liner, palletization, marks and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in a DAP RFQ?

Provide the food application, target standard and critical limits, quantity, packing, destination, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
