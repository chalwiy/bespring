What is Food Grade Encapsulated Sorbic Acid?

Application screening

Encapsulated Sorbic Acid food application screening

These are screening areas, not universal approvals, dosage recommendations or guaranteed outcomes. Confirm legal use and performance in the finished food.

Yeast-leavened bread

Bakery premixes

Controlled preservative delivery

Buyer questions

Encapsulated Sorbic Acid FAQ

What is Food Grade Encapsulated Sorbic Acid?

Food grade encapsulated sorbic acid is a lipid-coated preservative blend containing sorbic acid as the antimicrobial active and hydrogenated edible oil as the carrier. Encapsulation is evaluated where a modified release profile is needed. The current signed specification for the exact source governs supply.

What identity information applies to Encapsulated Sorbic Acid?

Encapsulated Sorbic Acid is described here as Active: 110-44-1, Active: 200 and Sorbic acid in edible lipid carrier. For blends, each component must be declared and qualified separately.

What specification should buyers request?

Request the current signed specification, agreed analytical methods, SDS, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve any missing method, unit or acceptance criterion before approval.

How is Encapsulated Sorbic Acid packed?

Confirm moisture-barrier bag, net weight, liner and pallets. Confirm liner, palletization, marks, gross weight and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in the RFQ?

Provide the application, target performance and limits, quantity, packing, destination market, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
