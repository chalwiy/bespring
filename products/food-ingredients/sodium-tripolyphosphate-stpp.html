Product overview

What is food grade STPP?

Food grade sodium tripolyphosphate is the pentasodium salt of triphosphoric acid. It is also known as pentasodium triphosphate and carries Codex INS number 451(i). The material is supplied as white granules or powder and is formulated into selected processed foods when phosphate functionality is required.

For procurement, “food grade” should never be treated as the whole specification. Buyers should also confirm assay, P2O5, insoluble matter, contaminant limits, particle form, test methods and destination-market documentation.

Why is STPP used in food processing?

STPP can provide several technical functions, but performance depends on the full phosphate system, raw material, process conditions and legal use level.

Moisture management

Can support water binding and processing yield in suitable protein-based formulations.

Texture support

Helps processors manage bite, consistency and finished-product texture in validated recipes.

Emulsification

Supports stable fat, protein and water systems in selected processed-food applications.

Sequestration

Binds certain metal ions that may otherwise interfere with product or process performance.

Buyer questions

Food grade STPP FAQ

What is food grade STPP used for?

It is used in permitted food applications where phosphate functionality is needed, including selected meat, poultry, seafood, dairy and processed-food formulations. The precise function and use level depend on the recipe, process and local regulations.

What is the food additive number for sodium tripolyphosphate?

Pentasodium triphosphate is identified by Codex INS number 451(i). Market-specific names and permitted applications can differ, so the buyer should verify destination-market requirements.

Is this specification the same as a batch COA?

No. This page presents the supplied reference limits. A certificate of analysis reports results for a particular batch and should be checked against the agreed purchase specification.

How is food grade STPP packed and loaded?

Standard packing is a net 25 kg kraft paper outer bag with an inner PE bag. Reference loading is 23 MT per 20GP without pallets or 18 MT with pallets, subject to shipment confirmation.

What information is needed for an STPP quotation?

Provide the required food standard, powder or granular preference, quantity, packaging, pallet requirement, destination port, application and delivery target.

Can I request product documents or a sample?

Ask the sales team for the specification, TDS, SDS, applicable quality documents and sample availability. State your application and destination so the relevant documents can be reviewed.
