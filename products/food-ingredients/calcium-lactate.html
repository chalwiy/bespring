Product identity

What is calcium lactate E327?

Calcium lactate is the calcium salt of lactic acid, identified as E327 in the EU and INS 327 by Codex. It may be supplied in anhydrous or hydrated form. Public specifications describe white crystalline powder or granules that are soluble in water and practically insoluble in ethanol.

It can serve as a firming agent, acidity regulator, flour treatment agent or source of calcium in permitted applications. Those functions describe possible technical roles—not blanket approval, a dosage recommendation or a nutrition claim.

The comparison buyers often miss

Hydrate form changes calcium content and delivered cost

EU purity criteria express calcium lactate as C6H10CaO6·nH2O, with n from 0 to 5. Water of crystallization changes formula weight, loss on drying and elemental-calcium percentage, even when two products meet an assay stated on an anhydrous basis.

Identity

Lock the hydration state

Request the stated form and loss-on-drying method. “Calcium lactate” alone is not enough for an equal-cost comparison.

Assay

Check the reporting basis

Separate “as supplied,” “dried basis” and “anhydrous basis.” Dry-basis assay does not reveal how much crystallization water is shipped.

Calcium

Normalize elemental calcium

Anhydrous calcium lactate contains about 18.37% theoretical calcium; pentahydrate about 13.00%. Use the offered grade's controlled data.

Economics

Compare usable calcium

Compare landed cost per kilogram of compliant product and, for fortification, per kilogram of delivered elemental calcium.

Formulation risk control

Solubility alone does not guarantee a clear, stable system

Calcium lactate may dissolve, yet free calcium can react with other ingredients. Test the finished matrix at manufacturing concentration and over shelf life.

01

Phosphates

Calcium and phosphate can reduce clarity or form deposits depending on species, pH, concentration and addition order.

02

Citrate & organic acids

Acid type and pH influence calcium speciation, taste and stability. Confirm the complete acid system.

03

Carbonate systems

Carbonate or bicarbonate may create haze, precipitation or gas/pH effects. Evaluate process sequence and temperature.

04

Alginate & hydrocolloids

Available calcium can trigger rapid gelation. Premixing and addition sequence determine whether the result is controlled or lumpy.

Useful bench protocolCompare a control and candidate grades at actual use level. Record dissolution time and temperature, pH, turbidity, sediment, viscosity or gel set, sensory response and stability after processing and storage.

Buyer questions

Calcium lactate FAQ

Is food grade calcium lactate the same as calcium lactate pentahydrate?

Not necessarily. Calcium lactate may be anhydrous or hydrated. State the hydration form, assay basis and loss-on-drying method.

How much elemental calcium does calcium lactate provide?

The theoretical value is about 18.37% for anhydrous calcium lactate and about 13.00% for pentahydrate. Use controlled grade data for formulation and labels.

Is calcium lactate soluble in water?

Public criteria describe it as soluble. Actual dissolution, clarity and stability depend on form, particle size, temperature, concentration, pH and other ingredients.

Can calcium lactate be used with sodium alginate?

Calcium can cross-link alginate. Addition order, concentration, contact time and sensory targets require trials.

Does E327 mean calcium lactate is allowed in every food?

No. E327 or INS 327 identifies the additive. Categories, conditions and labelling depend on the jurisdiction and finished food.

What should a calcium lactate RFQ include?

Provide hydrate form, standard, application, calcium target, critical properties, quantity, packing, destination, certificates and delivery date.
