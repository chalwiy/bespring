Direct product answer

What is food grade Sodium Alginate?

Food grade sodium alginate is a water-soluble alginate hydrocolloid identified by FAO/JECFA as INS 401 and CAS 9005-38-3. Grade selection depends strongly on viscosity, mesh and gel strength.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, application and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern a transaction.
