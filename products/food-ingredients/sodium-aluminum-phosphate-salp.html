Product overview

What is food grade SALP?

Food grade acidic sodium aluminum phosphate (SALP), also spelled sodium aluminium phosphate, is a raising agent identified as INS 541(i). It supplies acid for the sodium bicarbonate reaction that releases carbon dioxide in doughs and batters. JECFA acidic SALP specification.

The supplied pH and assay description identify this page as acidic SALP. The reference sheet names Na3Al2H15(PO4)8, the non-tetrahydrate composition (JECFA formula weight 897.82); its 15–16% ignition loss is consistent with that reference, but does not independently verify composition. JECFA also defines a tetrahydrate, NaAl3H14(PO4)8·4H2O (949.88). Require the offered grade's identity and assay basis in the signed specification.

Basic SALP, INS 541(ii), is a different emulsifying material and is not the leavening acid described here. The FDA identity record groups acidic and basic SALP under CAS 7785-88-8; that general number alone cannot distinguish them.

Formulation value

Why is SALP used in leavening systems?

Acidic SALP supplies acidity for bicarbonate-based leavening. Acid capacity and gas-release timing are separate purchase criteria; neither assay nor a generic SALP name defines the complete baking response.

Neutralizing value

NV is the mass of sodium bicarbonate neutralized by 100 parts by mass of leavening acid. Request the grade value, test method, sample basis and endpoint; no Bespring NV is specified here.

Reaction profile

Request gas release over time under stated mixing, holding and heating conditions. A slow or heat-responsive designation needs grade data; NV does not measure reaction speed.

Formula balance

Specify bicarbonate quantity and its weight basis, other acids, water availability and particle requirements. Check dispersion and dry-blend uniformity in the actual recipe.

Application trials

Match mixing temperature, hold time and bake or fry profile. Measure finished pH, volume, cell structure, color, flavor and texture against an approved control.

For definitions and formulation variables, see the American Society of Baking chemical leavening guide. Its generic descriptions are not a performance guarantee for the Bespring grade.

Compare SAPP reaction-rate grades and MCP monohydrate identity and leavening controls using each grade's NV and reaction method. Anhydrous DCP has a different identity and qualification path. These are not equal-weight substitutes. For a combined system, see bakery premix trial design, leavening phosphate selection and composition-controlled phosphate blends.

Market review: U.S. 21 CFR 182.1781 sets a good-manufacturing-practice condition for SALP. EU Regulation 380/2012 introduced a narrowly defined sponge-cake use for acidic E541; do not infer permission for general bakery or fried dough. Consult the current consolidated rules and Codex GSFA provisions for the intended category. SALP contains aluminum as part of its composition: a food-use limit expressed as aluminum is distinct from an impurity limit and from the mass of SALP added.

Buyer questions

Food grade SALP FAQ

Is this SALP acidic or basic?

This page covers the acidic leavening material described in the reference sheet, INS 541(i). Basic SALP, INS 541(ii), is a different emulsifying material. Confirm the offered composition and grade-specific identity in the specification and SDS.

What do INS 541(i), E541 and CAS 7785-88-8 mean?

INS 541(i) identifies acidic SALP; E541 is the EU designation for acidic sodium aluminium phosphate. FDA lists CAS 7785-88-8 as a general reference grouping acidic and basic SALP. These identifiers do not prove a particular composition, batch purity or permission in every food category.

Is neutralizing value the same as reaction rate?

No. NV expresses the mass of sodium bicarbonate neutralized by 100 parts by mass of leavening acid. Reaction rate describes gas release over time under defined conditions. Request both with their methods; this reference sheet provides neither a grade NV nor a measured reaction curve.

Can SALP replace SAPP or MCP at the same weight?

Do not assume equal-weight replacement. Use the offered grades’ verified neutralizing values, then compare reaction timing, finished pH and baking or frying results in the complete formula. Hydration form and physical grade also matter.

Does this reference specification demonstrate JECFA conformity?

No. Its arsenic maximum is 5 mg/kg versus the JECFA acidic SALP maximum of 3 mg/kg, and fluoride and several test details are not listed. Request an appropriate signed specification and batch results for the selected standard.

Is this specification the same as a batch COA?

No. The table contains commercial reference limits. A COA reports results for an identified lot, with the agreed methods and reporting basis. It must be checked against the approved purchase specification.

How is food grade SALP packed and loaded?

The existing SALP reference states 25 kg per bag, a 500 kg MOQ and 24 MT per 20-foot container. Reconfirm current order terms, bag/liner, pallet requirements and shipment payload before ordering.

What information is needed for an SALP quotation?

Provide the acidic grade, food standard, key limits, NV/reaction requirements, food application, bicarbonate basis and mix/hold/heating process. Include quantity, packaging, destination country/port, delivery terms and target date.

Can I request product documents or a sample?

Ask for the current specification, TDS, SDS, representative or batch COA and any certificates required for your market. Confirm document availability, product/site scope, validity and sample arrangements for the exact offered supply.
