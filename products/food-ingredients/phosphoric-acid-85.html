What is Food Grade Phosphoric Acid 85%?

Food grade phosphoric acid 85% is a concentrated aqueous solution of orthophosphoric acid used in permitted food processes for acidification, pH control, sequestration and related technical functions.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact formulation or source, current signed specification, intended function, complete food process and destination-market rules. A webpage is qualification guidance; the agreed specification and batch COA govern supply.

Buyer questions

Phosphoric Acid 85% FAQ

What is Food Grade Phosphoric Acid 85%?

Food grade phosphoric acid 85% is a concentrated aqueous solution of orthophosphoric acid used in permitted food processes for acidification, pH control, sequestration and related technical functions. The current signed specification for the exact source governs supply.

What identity information applies to Phosphoric Acid 85%?

Phosphoric Acid 85% is described here as 7664-38-2, 338 and H3PO4. For blends, each component must be declared and qualified separately.

What specification should buyers request?

Request the current signed specification, agreed analytical methods, SDS, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve any missing method, unit or acceptance criterion before approval.

How is Phosphoric Acid 85% packed?

Confirm drum, IBC or other approved packing. Confirm liner, palletization, marks, gross weight and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in the RFQ?

Provide the application, target performance and limits, quantity, packing, destination market, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
