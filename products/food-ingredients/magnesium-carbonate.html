Product overview

What is food grade Magnesium Carbonate?

Food grade magnesium carbonate is a light or heavy white magnesium carbonate material. The commercial reference describes a basic hydrated composition and 40.0–43.5% MgO, while Codex identifies magnesium carbonate as INS 504(i).

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, application and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern a transaction.
