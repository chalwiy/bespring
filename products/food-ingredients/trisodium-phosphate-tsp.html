Direct product answer

What is food grade trisodium phosphate?

Food grade trisodium phosphate (TSP), also called tribasic sodium phosphate, is INS 339(iii). The supplied sheet covers anhydrous Na3PO4 and dodecahydrate Na3PO4·12H2O forms.

This page separates the supplied commercial specification from independent identity references. Legal use, dosage and documentation must be confirmed for the destination market and exact food application.

Functional roles

Why is TSP used in food processing?

Performance depends on grade, dosage, formulation, process conditions and applicable food rules.

Function group 01

Alkaline buffering

TSP is alkaline and can influence formulation pH. Dose, taste, protein response and destination-market permissions must be assessed.

Function group 02

Sequestration and water management

The supplied information identifies water retention; recognized phosphate functions also include sequestration and emulsifying-salt use.

Food application screening

Common food grade TSP applications

These are application-screening areas, not universal use approvals or guaranteed outcomes.

Processed meat

Evaluate water retention, binding, yield, texture and sensory effects in the actual protein and salt system.

Processed cheese

May contribute to pH and mineral balance in qualified emulsifying-salt systems.

Dairy products

Screen protein interactions, buffering and stability while controlling alkalinity and flavor.

Beverages

Evaluate pH, clarity, mineral compatibility and taste before commercial use.

Dry ingredient blends

Confirm hydrate form, dissolution, flow and compatibility with acids and other minerals.

Sugar and process systems

The supplied information cites sugar-juice purification; food-contact and process use requires separate technical review.

Protect TSP from heat and moisture
