Direct product answer

What is food grade potassium tripolyphosphate?

Food grade potassium tripolyphosphate (KTPP) is a condensed potassium phosphate with formula K5P3O10 and CAS number 13845-36-8. The supplied grade is a white powder with a reference assay of at least 90.0%.

KTPP is not the same material as sodium tripolyphosphate (STPP) or tetrapotassium pyrophosphate (TKPP). Buyers should qualify the exact chemical identity, grade, specification and functional performance required by their process.

Functional roles

Why is KTPP evaluated in food formulations?

The supplied application information identifies several possible functions. Actual results depend on the complete phosphate system, dosage, raw materials, process and regulatory limits.

Function group 01

Emulsification and water management

KTPP may be evaluated where protein, fat and water interactions affect process yield and finished texture.

  • Emulsifier and binder roles
  • Moisture-retention support
  • Texture and microstructure modification
Function group 02

Sequestration and quality control

Phosphate functionality may support metal-ion control, stabilization and selected color or oxidative-stability objectives.

  • Chelating or sequestrant function
  • Stabilization and process control
  • Color-retention and antioxidant-system support

Food application screening

How should food manufacturers evaluate KTPP?

The supplied sheet describes KTPP by its functional roles rather than a closed list of food categories. Qualification should therefore start with the intended process and measurable acceptance criteria.

Protein systems

Screen emulsification, water retention, binding, yield and texture under the real salt, protein and temperature conditions.

Dairy and cheese

Evaluate mineral interactions, emulsification, stability, flavor and texture in the complete dairy formulation.

Seafood processing

Test moisture management, appearance and texture while controlling dosage, labeling and destination-market limits.

Phosphate blends

Compare KTPP alone and in permitted blends for solubility, pH, sequestration and functional performance.

Color stability

Measure color retention under realistic processing and storage; do not infer finished-product shelf life from ingredient identity alone.

Pilot validation

Define target outcomes, control formulation, sampling plan and test method before moving from bench to plant trial.

Buyer questions

Food grade KTPP FAQ

Is KTPP the same as TKPP?

No. KTPP is potassium tripolyphosphate, K5P3O10, CAS 13845-36-8. TKPP is tetrapotassium pyrophosphate, K4P2O7, CAS 7320-34-5. They require separate specifications and trials.

What is the reference KTPP assay?

The supplied sheet states K5P3O10 content of at least 90.0%. Confirm the current signed specification and test method for the offered batch.

What are the packing and minimum order?

The reference is 25 kg per bag, 22 metric tons per 20-foot container and a 500 kg minimum order. Confirm current pallet, liner and loading details.
