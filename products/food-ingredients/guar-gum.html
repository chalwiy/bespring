Direct product answer

What is food grade Guar Gum?

Food grade guar gum is produced mainly from the endosperm of guar seeds and consists primarily of high-molecular-weight galactomannan. FAO/JECFA identifies it as INS 412 and CAS 9000-30-0.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, application and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern a transaction.
