Product overview

What is food grade calcium citrate?

Reference assay97.5–100.5%

Standard packing25 kg bag

Container loading20 MT / 20' FCL

QualificationCOA and certificates on request

The supplied calcium citrate is identified as tetrahydrate Ca3(C6H5O7)2·4H2O, CAS 5785-44-4. Codex lists calcium citrate as INS 333(iii), while JECFA also records an anhydrous identity under a different CAS number.

The supplied commercial limits must be matched to the exact grade, intended food category and destination-market rules. A product name alone does not establish regulatory conformity.
