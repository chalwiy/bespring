Direct product answer

What is food grade monopotassium phosphate?

Food grade monopotassium phosphate (MKP), also called potassium dihydrogen phosphate or monobasic potassium phosphate, is an orthophosphate with formula KH2PO4 and CAS number 7778-77-0.

The supplied specification states at least 98.0% KH2PO4 on a dry basis. Food buyers should distinguish this grade from fertilizer or technical grades and qualify identity, impurities, documentation and application performance.

Food and fermentation uses

Where is food grade MKP used?

The supplied application guidance identifies culture, seasoning, yeast, leavening and fermentation roles. Technical suitability and legal status must be confirmed for the exact use.

Buffer systems

MKP can contribute phosphate buffering and potassium in suitable formulations where pH behavior must be controlled.

Microbial culture

May be used as a phosphate and potassium source in qualified bacterial-culture and bioprocess media.

Yeast culture

Evaluated in yeast cultivation and brewing-related culture systems as part of a complete nutrient program.

Seasoning manufacture

The supplied guidance identifies MKP in processes used to synthesize or produce selected seasoning materials.

Leavening systems

Can be assessed as a phosphate component in selected leavening formulations, with reaction behavior verified in the real recipe.

Fermentation aids

May support fermentation when phosphate, potassium and buffering needs are defined and validated at process scale.

Buyer questions

Food grade MKP FAQ

Is monopotassium phosphate the same as potassium dihydrogen phosphate?

Yes. Monopotassium phosphate, potassium dihydrogen phosphate and monobasic potassium phosphate are names for KH2PO4, CAS 7778-77-0.

How is food grade MKP different from fertilizer grade?

The chemical identity may be the same, but the applicable specification, impurities, controls, documentation, production or packing scope and regulatory suitability can differ. Require food-grade evidence for the offered source.

What is the reference assay?

The supplied food-grade sheet states at least 98.0% KH2PO4 on a dry basis. Confirm the current signed specification and batch COA.

What are the packing and minimum order?

The supplied reference is 25 kg per bag, 23 metric tons per 20-foot container and a 500 kg minimum order. Confirm current pallet, liner and loading details.

What details are needed for an MKP quotation?

State food grade, the intended application, target specification, quantity, packing, destination port, required documents and requested delivery date.
