Product identity

What is food grade monopotassium phosphate?

Monopotassium phosphate (MKP), also called potassium dihydrogen phosphate or monobasic potassium phosphate, is the acidic potassium orthophosphate KH2PO4. JECFA identifies it as INS 340(i), CAS 7778-77-0, with buffer, neutralizing-agent, sequestrant and yeast-food functions.

JECFA describes it as freely soluble in water with a pH of 4.2–4.7 for a 1% solution. It supplies potassium and phosphorus but does not provide sodium; any mineral or nutrition declaration must use the offered grade's certified composition and the complete finished-food formula.

The critical search and procurement boundary

Food grade MKP is not fertilizer MKP 0-52-34

The same chemical name and CAS appear in food and agricultural markets, but intended use, guaranteed analysis, impurity controls, manufacturing/packing scope and documentation are not interchangeable.

Application-specific screening

Where food grade MKP is evaluated

These are technical evaluation paths, not universal permissions or dosage guidance. Confirm the food category, total phosphate, mineral declaration and destination-market rules.

Beverage and electrolyte dry mixes

Evaluate MKP when a soluble potassium/phosphate input and acidic buffer profile are required. Check reconstitution pH, mineral taste, clarity, sediment, compatibility with calcium/magnesium and declared nutrition.

Food fermentation and cultures

Use as a candidate phosphate/potassium source and buffer only after defining organism, medium composition and target pH. Track growth, acidification, yield, by-products and downstream purification.

Nutrition powders and premixes

Calculate potassium and phosphorus from certified composition, then test particle-size compatibility, blend segregation, dissolution, caking, sensory impact and label limits.

Acidic phosphate buffer systems

Pairing MKP with a more alkaline potassium phosphate such as DKP can broaden pH adjustment options. Determine the ratio experimentally in the real ionic and temperature environment.

Choose the phosphate species

MKP vs. DKP and phosphoric acid

Selection begins with target pH and ion contribution, not with the shared word “potassium.” Equal-weight substitution changes potassium, phosphorus and acid/base balance.

MKP · KH2PO4

Acidic potassium orthophosphate

Use as a candidate where acidic phosphate buffering and potassium/phosphorus input are required. JECFA 1% pH: 4.2–4.7.

DKP · K2HPO4

More alkaline potassium orthophosphate

Compare when a higher pH response or different potassium-to-phosphate ratio is required. A controlled MKP/DKP ratio can be evaluated as a buffer pair.

Phosphoric acid · H3PO4

Acidulant without potassium input

Compare when acidification is the primary goal and added potassium is not desired. Handling, concentration and corrosion controls differ.

Theoretical composition for preliminary calculationsPure anhydrous KH2PO4 contains approximately 28.7% potassium and 22.8% phosphorus by mass. Use certified batch composition—not theoretical purity—for labels and commercial formulation.

Buyer questions

Food grade MKP FAQ

Is monopotassium phosphate the same as potassium dihydrogen phosphate?

Yes. Monopotassium phosphate, potassium dihydrogen phosphate and monobasic potassium phosphate identify KH2PO4. For food use, specify INS/E340(i), CAS 7778-77-0 and the required food standard.

How is food grade MKP different from fertilizer MKP 0-52-34?

They can share the same chemical formula, but fertilizer grade is controlled and labeled for plant nutrition. Food grade requires food-additive identity, appropriate impurities, traceability, packing and applicable food documents. An NPK certificate does not qualify a food ingredient.

Is MKP acidic or alkaline?

MKP is the acidic potassium orthophosphate. JECFA specifies pH 4.2–4.7 for a 1% solution. Actual formulation pH depends on concentration and the full buffer, mineral and acid system.

Does the supplied specification meet JECFA?

Not as written: the supplied lead limit is 5 mg/kg versus JECFA's 4 mg/kg maximum, and several JECFA identity/quality fields are absent. Request a source-specific grade and COA that meet the selected standard.

What details are needed for an MKP quotation?

State food grade, intended application, target pH, potassium/phosphorus or K2O/P2O5 basis, specification, particle/solution requirements, quantity, packing, destination, Incoterm and documents.
