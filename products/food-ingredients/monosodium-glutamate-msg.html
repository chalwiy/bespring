What is food grade Monosodium Glutamate?

Food grade monosodium L-glutamate (MSG), INS 621, is the sodium salt of L-glutamic acid used as an umami flavour enhancer in suitable savoury foods and seasoning systems.

For procurement, “food grade” must be supported by a current source-specific specification, suitable manufacturing and quality documents, and a batch COA. Final use depends on the complete formulation, intended function and destination-market rules.

Buyer questions

Food grade Monosodium Glutamate FAQ

What is food grade Monosodium Glutamate used for?

Monosodium Glutamate is used in suitable food processes for umami flavour enhancer functions. Legal use, dosage and performance must be confirmed for the exact food and destination market.

What are the CAS and food additive numbers for Monosodium Glutamate?

Monosodium Glutamate is identified here by CAS 6106-04-3 and INS 621. Confirm the identity and supplied form on the current specification.

What specification should a buyer request for Monosodium Glutamate?

Request the current signed specification, agreed test methods, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve every missing unit, comparator or particle-size requirement before approval.

How is food grade Monosodium Glutamate packed?

Confirm bag type, net weight and particle-size option. Confirm food-contact liner, palletization, marks and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in a MSG RFQ?

Provide the food application, target standard and critical limits, quantity, packing, destination, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
