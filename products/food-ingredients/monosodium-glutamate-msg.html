Buyer questions

Food grade Monosodium Glutamate FAQ

What is INS 621?

INS 621 is monosodium L-glutamate, commonly called MSG. It is used as an umami flavor enhancer in permitted savory food applications; E621 is the corresponding European designation.

Is MSG the same as table salt?

No. MSG is a sodium salt of glutamic acid, while table salt is sodium chloride. Their taste and composition differ, so salt-reduction work requires sensory testing and a calculation for the complete recipe.

Which MSG crystal size should buyers choose?

Select crystal size against dry-blend particle matching, dosing, dissolution and appearance requirements. The reference specification on this page does not define particle size; agree it explicitly with the source.

What documents should buyers request?

Request the current signed specification, analytical methods and representative and batch COAs. Confirm identity, assay, specific rotation, clarity and the relevant impurity limits for the selected grade.

What should an MSG RFQ include?

Provide the seasoning or food application, crystal-size requirement, target standard and critical limits, quantity, packing, destination, certificates and delivery terms.
