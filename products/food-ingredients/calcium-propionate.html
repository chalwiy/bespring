Product overview

What is food grade calcium propionate?

Calcium propionate, also called calcium propanoate, is the calcium salt of propionic acid. It is identified as E282 in the European food-additive numbering system and INS 282 in Codex references. Food grade material is typically supplied as white crystals, powder or granules with no more than a faint propionic-acid odour.

In suitable food systems, dissolved propionate helps inhibit mould and rope-forming microorganisms. Practical performance depends on product pH, water activity, process hygiene, dosage, ingredient distribution, packaging and the target organism. It is one part of a preservation system—not a substitute for sanitation, process control or shelf-life validation.

From ingredient to shelf life

Design a bakery preservation trial around the full system

A useful calcium propionate trial separates preservative performance from process and packaging effects. Define a control, document the variables below and assess repeat batches under realistic storage and distribution conditions.

01

Formula environment

Record dough and finished-product pH, moisture and water activity. These conditions influence the preservative system and microbial growth.

02

Process performance

Monitor mixing, fermentation, proof time, bake profile, loaf volume and sensory effects rather than treating shelf life as the only result.

03

Hygiene & cooling

Control post-bake exposure, cooling time and contact surfaces. Recontamination can obscure the effect of a formulation change.

04

Package & distribution

Use the intended package and challenge realistic storage temperature, transport time and opening conditions across repeat batches.

The values above illustrate one supplied result set. They do not establish the limits for FCC material, blended products or future shipments. Approve the applicable specification first, then compare each shipment COA with that agreed document.

Specification note: The supplied “Pb²⁺ (Heavy metals)” item has been presented as “Heavy metals (as Pb)” for clarity. Confirm the analytical method and whether a separate lead limit is required by your customer standard or destination market.

Supplied table versus the JECFA monograph

These are different references and should not be treated as interchangeable. JECFA lists an assay of at least 98.0% on the dried basis, water-insoluble matter no more than 0.3%, fluoride no more than 30 mg/kg, lead no more than 5 mg/kg and iron no more than 50 mg/kg. The supplied table uses a tighter 99.0–100.5% assay range and 0.2% insoluble-matter limit, but reports “heavy metals (as Pb)” rather than a separate lead test and does not show iron. Ask which standard, analytical methods and contaminant limits apply to the offered lot.

Buyer questions

Calcium propionate FAQ

What is food grade calcium propionate used for?

It is used as a preservative, antimould and antirope ingredient. It is especially associated with bread and baked goods, and may also be used in other food categories where destination-market regulations permit.

How does calcium propionate help preserve bread?

In a suitable formulation, propionate ions help inhibit mould and rope-forming microorganisms. Results depend on pH, moisture, dosage, process, distribution and the microbial challenge, so shelf-life trials remain essential.

What is the difference between E282 food grade and FCC calcium propionate?

E282 or INS 282 identifies calcium propionate as a food additive. FCC refers to a Food Chemicals Codex specification. State the required FCC edition, customer limits and destination market so sales can confirm the correct product and documents.

Is blended calcium propionate available?

Yes. Blended calcium propionate is available for different customer and application needs. Contact sales with your formulation, process, dosing method, target shelf life and acceptance criteria so the appropriate composition can be discussed.

Can I request a specification, COA, TDS or SDS?

Yes. Request the documents applicable to the exact standard or blended product being evaluated. A shipment COA should be checked against the agreed purchase specification.

What information is needed for an accurate quotation?

Provide the required grade or blend, specification, quantity, packaging, destination country or port, intended food application and target delivery date.
