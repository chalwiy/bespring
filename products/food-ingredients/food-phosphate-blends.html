Direct product answer

What is Custom Food Phosphate Blends?

Custom food phosphate blends combine selected permitted phosphate ingredients to meet an agreed formula or processing target. Attachment 10 states that the product can be customized according to the customer’s formulation.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact formulation or source, current signed specification, intended function, complete food process and destination-market rules. A webpage is qualification guidance; the agreed specification and batch COA govern supply.

Buyer questions

Phosphate Blends FAQ

What is Custom Food Phosphate Blends?

Custom food phosphate blends combine selected permitted phosphate ingredients to meet an agreed formula or processing target. Attachment 10 states that the product can be customized according to the customer’s formulation. The current signed specification for the exact source governs supply.

What identity information applies to Phosphate Blends?

Phosphate Blends is described here as No single CAS, Composition-specific and Custom mixture. For blends, each component must be declared and qualified separately.

What specification should buyers request?

Request the current signed specification, agreed analytical methods, SDS, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve any missing method, unit or acceptance criterion before approval.

How is Phosphate Blends packed?

Confirm blend, bag, liner, net weight and pallet requirement. Confirm liner, palletization, marks, gross weight and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in the RFQ?

Provide the application, target performance and limits, quantity, packing, destination market, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
