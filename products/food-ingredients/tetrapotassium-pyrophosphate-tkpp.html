Product overview

What is tetrapotassium pyrophosphate?

Tetrapotassium pyrophosphate (TKPP), also called tetrapotassium diphosphate or potassium pyrophosphate, is the potassium salt of pyrophosphoric acid. Its chemical formula is K4P2O7, its CAS number is 7320-34-5, and its food additive identity is INS 450(v) / E450(v). This page describes solid TKPP with the anhydrous formula shown, not a liquid solution or a specified hydrate.

TKPP is hygroscopic and soluble in water. The JECFA TKPP monograph identifies emulsifier and texturizer functions. Its formula weight is 330.34 g/mol. Confirm the offered grade’s form and reporting basis; absorbed moisture is not evidence of a defined crystalline hydrate. Food qualification requires the relevant specification and documents, rather than chemical identity alone.

Potassium-based phosphate decision

When should a formulator evaluate TKPP?

Evaluate TKPP when potassium-based pyrophosphate functionality addresses a defined formulation requirement. Compare sodium and potassium contribution, solution behavior and measured finished-food performance with the current phosphate system.

01

Sodium contribution

The K₄P₂O₇ formula contains no sodium; sodium impurities in the offered grade need separate confirmation, so it may be screened against TSPP in sodium-reduction work. This does not make the finished food “low sodium”; calculate every sodium source and verify the legal claim criteria.

02

Potassium load

Pure anhydrous K4P2O7 is theoretically about 47.4% potassium by mass. This formula calculation is not a batch assay. Request composition data for the offered grade and distinguish elemental K and P from K₂O or P₂O₅ reporting.

03

Alkalinity and flavor

JECFA gives pH 10.0–10.7 for a 1 in 100 solution. This is a standard reference, not a published Bespring pH guarantee or the final food pH. Measure the complete formulation and assess flavor, color and texture under the intended process.

04

Dissolution environment

Although TKPP is water-soluble, practical dissolution depends on concentration, temperature, water hardness, agitation, salt content and ingredient order. Check clarity and sediment in the actual brine or process water.

Claim boundary: replacing a sodium phosphate with TKPP can reduce sodium contributed by that ingredient, but nutritional claims require a calculation for the complete finished food and the destination market.

Functional roles

Why is TKPP used in food processing?

The FDA potassium pyrophosphate inventory lists emulsifier/emulsifier-salt and texturizer effects, consistent with the JECFA monograph. These functions describe the ingredient; they do not establish performance or permission for every food.

Function 01

Mineral and pH management

Pyrophosphate can interact with polyvalent ions such as calcium. TKPP also gives an alkaline aqueous solution. Sequestration and pH adjustment are distinct effects; evaluate mineral balance, protein behavior, clarity and sediment separately in the complete system.

  • Metal-ion sequestration
  • Check water hardness and calcium load
  • Measure solution and finished-product pH

Function 02

Protein and emulsion functionality

In suitable meat or cheese systems, an alkaline phosphate system can support protein hydration and fat–water stabilization. Raw material, salt, shear, temperature and total phosphate determine the result.

  • Emulsifier and texturizer functionality
  • Protein extraction and hydration screening
  • Cook loss, purge and texture validation

Food industry uses

Common food grade TKPP applications

Use these application areas to define a trial brief, then confirm the exact food category and permitted use in the destination market. A reference specification does not predict retained yield, texture or storage stability.

Meat and poultry brines

Compare TKPP within a phosphate system when sodium contribution, protein extraction, water binding or emulsion stability is under review. Record brine clarity, pickup, cook loss, chilled purge, texture and sensory quality.

Processed cheese systems

Screen calcium sequestration, casein hydration and fat–water emulsification. Control natural-cheese composition, calcium, moisture, pH, heating, shear and cooling before judging slice or spread texture.

Seafood processing

Where permitted, compare TKPP or a phosphate blend for solution behavior and retained yield. Measure pickup after equal drainage, thaw/cook loss, texture and retained phosphate where required. Follow the TKPP frozen-seafood trial guide for species and process variables.

Potassium-based phosphate blends

Use TKPP as one component only when its alkalinity and pyrophosphate functionality help meet a defined sodium, solubility or performance target. Validate blend compatibility and storage stability.

Specification decision: The commercial fluoride maximum is 30 mg/kg, compared with 10 mg/kg in JECFA and the published EU E450(v) text. That limit cannot guarantee either requirement, although an individual batch may test lower. Published EU arsenic and lead maxima are each 1 mg/kg, below the commercial 3 and 2 mg/kg limits. Request an aligned specification, methods and batch COA; matching selected limits does not establish full conformity.

Test-basis distinction: The commercial reference is “Loss on ignition at 110°C” ≤0.5%. JECFA ignition loss is ≤2% using 105°C for 4 hours, then 550°C for 30 minutes. The commercial label does not establish that method and must not be treated as a loss-on-drying result. Assay, ignition loss and P₂O₅ are separate measurements.

JECFA identity and specification

JECFA lists INS 450(v), CAS 7320-34-5, formula K4P2O7, formula weight 330.34 g/mol, assay ≥95% on the ignited basis and pH 10.0–10.7 for a 1 in 100 solution.

EU specification context

The published Regulation (EU) No 231/2012 text specifies E450(v) P2O5 42.0–43.7% on the anhydrous basis. This is a standard requirement, not a Bespring commercial value. Check the latest consolidated text and amendments before market approval.

Avoid phosphate-name confusion

TKPP vs. TSPP and KTPP

These materials are not interchangeable by acronym or equal weight. Compare the ion, phosphate chain, alkalinity, solubility, legal identity and validated function.

TKPP

K4P2O7 · INS 450(v)

Potassium pyrophosphate; consider when potassium-based, alkaline pyrophosphate functionality is required.

TSPP

Na4P2O7 · INS 450(iii)

Sodium pyrophosphate; compare sodium contribution, practical dissolution and equivalent functional performance.

Buyer questions

Food grade TKPP FAQ

Why is TKPP evaluated instead of TSPP?

TKPP supplies potassium rather than the sodium supplied by TSPP. Compare the full formula’s sodium and potassium contribution, pH, dissolution, flavor and process results. An equal-weight substitution or a finished-food low-sodium claim must not be assumed.

Is TKPP the same as tetrapotassium diphosphate?

Yes. Tetrapotassium pyrophosphate, tetrapotassium diphosphate and potassium pyrophosphate identify K₄P₂O₇, CAS 7320-34-5, INS 450(v) / E450(v). This page describes the solid material; a liquid solution requires a separately specified concentration.

Does TKPP make a finished food low sodium?

No. Its chemical formula contains no sodium, but the offered grade may have impurities and the food contains other sodium sources. Use supported composition data for the complete finished food and verify destination-market claim criteria.

What must be checked before dissolving TKPP?

Specify the offered powder or granular form, water temperature and hardness, target concentration, agitation, salt/mineral content and ingredient order. Measure dissolution time, clarity, sediment and solution pH in the actual process water or brine.

Does the supplied specification meet JECFA or EU limits?

It cannot guarantee conformity. The fluoride limit is 30 mg/kg, compared with 10 mg/kg in JECFA and the published EU E450(v) text. The published EU arsenic and lead limits are also lower. A batch may test below the commercial maximum; request an aligned specification, confirmed methods and a batch COA, and check the current applicable standard.

What details are needed for an accurate TKPP quotation?

Provide solid food grade TKPP, powder/granular preference, application and process, destination market, required standard, assay basis, fluoride and other critical limits, quantity, packaging, port, Incoterm and delivery requirements. List the TDS, SDS, COA and declarations needed for approval.

Is food grade TKPP interchangeable with technical or fertilizer grade?

No. The chemical name and CAS do not establish food qualification. Impurity controls, intended use, documentation and applicable rules differ. Specify food grade and the required standard; do not qualify a technical, fertilizer or liquid product from identity alone.

Is the 110°C loss value the same as JECFA loss on ignition?

No equivalence is established. The commercial reference states “Loss on ignition at 110°C” ≤0.5%, while JECFA uses 105°C for 4 hours followed by 550°C for 30 minutes, with a ≤2% limit. Agree the analytical method and reporting basis; do not rename the commercial result loss on drying.
