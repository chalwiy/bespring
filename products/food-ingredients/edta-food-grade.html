Buyer-intent guide

How to source Food Grade EDTA in bulk

Use these long-tail procurement questions when comparing a manufacturer source, China supplier, distributor offer, sample or bulk quotation.

food grade EDTA supplier

Name the exact salt, CAS or INS identity, application, quantity and destination market.

calcium disodium EDTA food grade

For INS 385, confirm CAS 62-33-9 and the relevant permitted food category.

disodium EDTA food grade bulk

For INS 386, confirm CAS 139-33-3 and do not substitute it for calcium disodium EDTA.

EDTA sequestrant for food processing

Describe the metal-control objective, formula, process and target shelf-life evidence.

Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Food Grade EDTA

Exact salt

The RFQ must say calcium disodium EDTA, disodium EDTA or another exact compound—not EDTA alone.

Identity numbers

Match CAS and INS numbers to the salt; free acid and salts have different identities.

Legal permission

EDTA permissions are food-category and jurisdiction specific; verify before formulation.

Technical need

Define which metal ions or stability problem the sequestrant is expected to address.
