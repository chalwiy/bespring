Product overview

What is food grade EDTA?

EDTA is a family name for ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid and its salts. Food procurement must identify the exact compound: calcium disodium EDTA is INS 385, while disodium EDTA is INS 386; free EDTA acid must not be treated as interchangeable with either salt.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact source, intended function, full food process and destination-market rules. Agree the grade-specific specification for your order and use the batch COA for acceptance.

Procurement checklist

How to source Food Grade EDTA in bulk

Compare the offered grade, application requirements and commercial terms before requesting a sample or placing an order.

Specify the exact EDTA salt

Name the exact salt, CAS or INS identity, application, quantity and destination market.

Calcium disodium EDTA — INS 385

For INS 385, confirm CAS 62-33-9 and the relevant permitted food category.

Disodium EDTA — INS 386

For INS 386, confirm CAS 139-33-3 and do not substitute it for calcium disodium EDTA.

Define the metal-control objective

Describe the metal-control objective, formula, process and target shelf-life evidence.

Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Food Grade EDTA

Exact salt

The RFQ must say calcium disodium EDTA, disodium EDTA or another exact compound—not EDTA alone.

Identity numbers

Match CAS and INS numbers to the salt; free acid and salts have different identities.

Legal permission

EDTA permissions are food-category and jurisdiction specific; verify before formulation.

Technical need

Define which metal ions or stability problem the sequestrant is expected to address.

Food Grade EDTA FAQ

What is food grade EDTA?

EDTA is a family name for ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid and its salts. Food procurement must identify the exact compound: calcium disodium EDTA is INS 385, while disodium EDTA is INS 386; free EDTA acid must not be treated as interchangeable with either salt. The current signed specification for the selected source governs supply.

Which identity should buyers use for Food Grade EDTA?

Exact EDTA salt required; INS depends on salt; Source-specific EDTA compound. Where the name covers a family or mixture, confirm the exact member and composition before approval.

Why is there no public Food Grade EDTA specification on this page?

For the offered grade, request the current specification or TDS, relevant test methods and available COA documentation. Confirm the agreed quality requirements before ordering.

What documents are available for Food Grade EDTA?

Ask for the current specification or TDS, SDS, representative COA, batch COA and relevant food-safety, origin, allergen, Kosher, Halal or other certificates for the exact source.

What should a Food Grade EDTA RFQ include?

Provide the exact grade or type, application, critical performance needs, quantity, packing, destination market, required documents, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
