Choose a yogurt starter culture

A yogurt starter culture is a preparation of lactic acid bacteria used to ferment a milk base. HU2 and IU2 are freeze-dried cultures for direct vat inoculation; the appropriate choice depends on the finished yogurt and processing conditions. Yogurt and yoghurt are alternative spellings.
