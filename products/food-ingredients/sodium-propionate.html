What is food grade sodium propionate?

Food grade sodium propionate, also called sodium propanoate, is INS 281, CAS 137-40-6 and formula C3H5O2Na.

The supplied commercial limits must be matched to the exact grade, intended food category and destination-market rules. A product name alone does not establish regulatory conformity.
