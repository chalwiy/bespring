Direct product answer

What is food grade Monoammonium Phosphate?

Food grade monoammonium phosphate (MAP), chemically ammonium dihydrogen phosphate, is a water-soluble acidic phosphate used in suitable foods as a buffer, dough conditioner and leavening ingredient.

For procurement, “food grade” must be supported by a current source-specific specification, suitable manufacturing and quality documents, and a batch COA. Final use depends on the complete formulation, intended function and destination-market rules.

Buyer questions

Food grade Monoammonium Phosphate FAQ

What is food grade Monoammonium Phosphate used for?

Monoammonium Phosphate is used in suitable food processes for phosphate, buffer and leavening ingredient functions. Legal use, dosage and performance must be confirmed for the exact food and destination market.

What are the CAS and food additive numbers for Monoammonium Phosphate?

Monoammonium Phosphate is identified here by CAS 7722-76-1 and INS 342(i). Confirm the identity and supplied form on the current specification.

What specification should a buyer request for Monoammonium Phosphate?

Request the current signed specification, agreed test methods, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve every missing unit, comparator or particle-size requirement before approval.

How is food grade Monoammonium Phosphate packed?

Confirm bag type, net weight and pallet requirement. Confirm food-contact liner, palletization, marks and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in a MAP RFQ?

Provide the food application, target standard and critical limits, quantity, packing, destination, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
