Direct product answer

What is food grade disodium phosphate?

Food grade disodium phosphate (DSP), formally disodium hydrogen phosphate, is INS 339(ii). CAS 7558-79-4 corresponds to anhydrous Na2HPO4; hydrated forms require separate formula and identity confirmation.

This page separates the supplied commercial specification from independent identity references. Legal use, dosage and documentation must be confirmed for the destination market and exact food application.

Functional roles

Why is DSP used in food processing?

Performance depends on grade, dosage, formulation, process conditions and applicable food rules.

Function group 01

Buffering and acidity regulation

DSP can help establish or maintain pH in compatible cheese, beverage, jelly, dairy and ingredient systems.

Function group 02

Water and protein management

The supplied information identifies water retention; evaluate protein interactions, texture and stability in the actual formulation.

Food application screening

Common food grade DSP applications

These are application-screening areas, not universal use approvals or guaranteed outcomes.

Processed cheese

May be used in qualified emulsifying-salt systems to support pH, mineral balance, melt and texture.

Beverages

Evaluate buffering, clarity, taste and compatibility with calcium, proteins and acids.

Jelly and desserts

Screen acidity regulation, gel interaction and storage stability in the complete recipe.

Ice cream

May support buffering and water management after compatibility trials with the dairy and stabilizer system.

Processed meat

Evaluate water retention, binding and yield under the actual salt, protein and heat process.

Culture media

The supplied information cites culture use; confirm grade, nutrient role and process requirements.
