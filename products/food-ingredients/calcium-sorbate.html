Direct product answer

What is food grade Calcium Sorbate?

Food grade calcium sorbate, INS 203, is the calcium salt of sorbic acid used as a preservative or antimould ingredient in food categories where local regulations permit.

For procurement, “food grade” must be supported by a current source-specific specification, suitable manufacturing and quality documents, and a batch COA. Final use depends on the complete formulation, intended function and destination-market rules.

Buyer questions

Food grade Calcium Sorbate FAQ

What is food grade Calcium Sorbate used for?

Calcium Sorbate is used in suitable food processes for sorbate preservative and antimould ingredient functions. Legal use, dosage and performance must be confirmed for the exact food and destination market.

What are the CAS and food additive numbers for Calcium Sorbate?

Calcium Sorbate is identified here by CAS 7492-55-9 and INS 203. Confirm the identity and supplied form on the current specification.

What specification should a buyer request for Calcium Sorbate?

Request the current signed specification, agreed test methods, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve every missing unit, comparator or particle-size requirement before approval.

How is food grade Calcium Sorbate packed?

Confirm bag or drum, liner, net weight and pallets. Confirm food-contact liner, palletization, marks and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in a E203 RFQ?

Provide the food application, target standard and critical limits, quantity, packing, destination, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
