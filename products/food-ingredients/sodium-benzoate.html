Direct product answer

What is food grade Sodium Benzoate?

Food grade sodium benzoate is the sodium salt of benzoic acid, identified as INS 211. Its antimicrobial effectiveness is strongly influenced by finished-product pH, formulation, process, packaging and the target microorganisms.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, intended function and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern each transaction.

Application screening

Common food grade Sodium Benzoate applications

These are formulation areas rather than universal approvals or dosage recommendations. Confirm legal use and performance in the complete finished food.

Fruit juice and soft drinks

Used in suitable acidic beverages after pH, flavor, process and local-limit validation.

Sauces and condiments

Helps control yeasts, molds and selected bacteria in compatible acidic systems.

Pickles and relishes

Works as one element of an acid, salt, heat and packaging preservation program.

Jams and fruit preparations

May support shelf-life control where the recipe and regulatory category permit.

Canned and prepared foods

Validate target organisms, thermal process and package integrity; preservative addition is not a substitute for process control.

Bakery fillings

Can be evaluated for suitable acidic fillings rather than assumed effective in all baked products.
