Direct product answer

What is food grade tetrasodium pyrophosphate?

Food grade tetrasodium pyrophosphate (TSPP), also called tetrasodium diphosphate, is INS 450(iii). The supplied anhydrous grade is identified as Na4P2O7, CAS 7722-88-5.

This page separates the supplied commercial specification from independent identity references. Legal use, dosage and documentation must be confirmed for the destination market and exact food application.

Functional roles

Why is TSPP used in food processing?

Performance depends on grade, dosage, formulation, process conditions and applicable food rules.

Function group 01

Emulsification and buffering

JECFA recognizes emulsifier and buffering functions. TSPP can influence pH, protein behavior and emulsion stability.

Function group 02

Sequestration and water management

TSPP can bind selected metal ions and is used in suitable dairy, meat, cheese and beverage formulations.

Food application screening

Common food grade TSPP applications

These are application-screening areas, not universal use approvals or guaranteed outcomes.

Processed meat

Evaluate water retention, binding, yield, texture and phosphate balance in the actual protein and salt system.

Processed cheese

May support emulsification, mineral balance, melt and texture when used in a qualified emulsifying-salt system.

Dairy products

Screen buffering, water management and protein interactions in the complete formulation.

Beverages

Evaluate pH, clarity, taste and mineral compatibility before commercial use.

Dry blends

Confirm flow, dissolution sequence and compatibility with acids, proteins and other mineral salts.

Compound phosphates

TSPP may be blended with other permitted phosphates after performance and regulatory review.
