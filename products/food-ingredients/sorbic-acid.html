Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Sorbic Acid

pH and efficacy

Undissociated sorbic acid drives antimicrobial activity, so product pH is a central design input.

Solubility choice

Compare sorbic acid with sorbate salts where water solubility and addition point matter.

Shelf-life evidence

Use challenge or shelf-life trials in the finished food; ingredient identity alone is not a guarantee.

Regulatory review

Confirm permitted foods, maximum levels and labeling in each sales market.
