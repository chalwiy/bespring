Product overview

What is food grade Apple Cider Vinegar?

Apple cider vinegar is a fermented apple ingredient produced through alcoholic and acetous fermentation. It is not a single chemical additive, so buyers should qualify form, declared acidity, apple source, filtration or pasteurization, carrier ingredients for powders and sensory profile.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact source, intended function, full food process and destination-market rules. Agree the grade-specific specification for your order and use the batch COA for acceptance.
