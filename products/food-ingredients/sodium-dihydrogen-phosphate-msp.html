Product overview

What is food grade sodium dihydrogen phosphate?

Reference assay98.0–103.0%

Standard packing25 kg bag

Container loading24 MT / 20' FCL

QualificationCOA and certificates on request

Food grade sodium dihydrogen phosphate (MSP), also called monosodium phosphate, is INS 339(i). CAS 7558-80-7 identifies anhydrous NaH2PO4; hydrated forms must be specified separately.

Commercial specifications and independent additive-identity references serve different purposes. Legal use, dosage and documentation must be confirmed for the destination market and exact food application.

Functional roles

Why is MSP used in food processing?

Performance depends on grade, dosage, formulation, process conditions and applicable food rules.

Function group 01

Acidity regulation and buffering

MSP is the acidic member of the sodium orthophosphate series and can contribute to pH control in compatible formulations.

Function group 02

Emulsification and water management

Evaluate emulsifying-salt and water-management functions in compatible cheese, meat, beverage and jelly formulations.

Food application screening

Common food grade MSP applications

These are application-screening areas, not universal use approvals or guaranteed outcomes.

Processed cheese

Evaluate acidity, emulsifying-salt balance, melt and texture in the complete cheese formulation.

Processed meat

Screen water retention, pH response, binding and yield under actual protein and salt conditions.

Beverages

Evaluate pH, taste, clarity and compatibility with minerals, proteins and other acids.

Jelly and desserts

Assess acidity regulation, gel behavior and shelf stability in the intended recipe.

Dry blends

Confirm hydrate form, flow, dissolution and moisture pickup in powdered ingredient systems.

Phosphate blends

Compare MSP alone and in permitted phosphate combinations for target pH and functional performance.

Specification note: NaH2PO4 is anhydrous, not monohydrate; NaH2PO4·2H2O is the dihydrate. The 10–15% loss-on-drying reference is labeled dihydrate but does not establish the correct hydration form or test method. Storage and handling

Protect MSP from heat and moisture

  • TemperatureStore at no more than 30°C under the stated storage conditions.
  • EnvironmentKeep sealed in a cool, dry, ventilated place away from sunlight, heat, humidity and contamination.
  • Workplace controlsFollow the current SDS and site procedures for dust control, hygiene and personal protection.
