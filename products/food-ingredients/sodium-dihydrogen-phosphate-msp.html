Direct product answer

What is food grade sodium dihydrogen phosphate?

Food grade sodium dihydrogen phosphate (MSP), also called monosodium phosphate, is INS 339(i). CAS 7558-80-7 identifies anhydrous NaH2PO4; hydrated forms must be specified separately.

This page separates the supplied commercial specification from independent identity references. Legal use, dosage and documentation must be confirmed for the destination market and exact food application.

Functional roles

Why is MSP used in food processing?

Performance depends on grade, dosage, formulation, process conditions and applicable food rules.

Function group 01

Acidity regulation and buffering

MSP is the acidic member of the sodium orthophosphate series and can contribute to pH control in compatible formulations.

Function group 02

Emulsification and water management

The supplied information identifies emulsifying and water-retention roles in cheese, meat, beverage and jelly applications.
