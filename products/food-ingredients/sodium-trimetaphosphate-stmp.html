Direct product answer

What is food grade sodium trimetaphosphate?

Food grade sodium trimetaphosphate (STMP) is a cyclic condensed phosphate listed in the supplied sheet as Na3(PO3)3, CAS 7785-84-4, with a reference content of at least 98.5%.

This page separates the supplied commercial specification from independent identity references. Legal use, dosage and documentation must be confirmed for the destination market and exact food application.

Functional roles

Why is STMP used in food processing?

Performance depends on grade, dosage, formulation, process conditions and applicable food rules.

Function group 01

Starch and texture systems

STMP is used in selected modified-starch processes and can be evaluated for texture, viscosity and process stability.

Function group 02

Water and dispersion management

The supplied application information identifies water holding, softening, adhesion, dispersion and stabilization functions.

Food application screening

Common food grade STMP applications

These are application-screening areas, not universal use approvals or guaranteed outcomes.

Starch modification

Used as a phosphate reagent in qualifying modified-starch processes; confirm residual limits and the applicable food standard.

Fruit juice and beverages

Evaluate water holding, haze control, mineral interactions, color and vitamin stability in the complete beverage system.

Meat processing

Screen water management, binding, dispersion and yield under the actual protein, salt and process conditions.

Ice cream

May support stabilization objectives after checking compatibility with proteins, minerals, hydrocolloids and freezing conditions.

Cheese and dairy

Evaluate dispersion, mineral balance, texture and storage stability in the intended dairy formulation.

Color and vitamin protection

The supplied information cites reduced discoloration and vitamin C decomposition; validate these outcomes in shelf-life trials.
