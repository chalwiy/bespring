These are screening areas, not universal approvals, dosage recommendations or guaranteed outcomes. Confirm legal use and technical performance in the finished food.

Evaluate wetting, dispersibility, sediment and heat stability. Include the actual pH, minerals and homogenization conditions in the trial.

Texture and structure in prepared foods

Define the required gel, bite or water-binding performance. Compare the selected protein in the complete recipe and processing sequence.

Technical selection

Questions to resolve before approving Faba Protein

Protein source and product category

Specify the botanical or dairy source and whether the product is flour, concentrate, isolate or textured material. Confirm any blends or standardizing ingredients.

Protein basis, solubility and functionality

Agree protein method and moisture basis alongside application tests. Protein percentage alone does not establish solubility, gelling or emulsifying performance.

Flavor, color and particle properties

Use approved reference samples and appropriate analytical targets. Assess the sensory and physical properties at the actual inclusion in the finished food.

Allergens, microbiology and origin claims

Require current source-specific allergen, microbiological and origin evidence. Verify any certification claim for the exact product and manufacturing site.
