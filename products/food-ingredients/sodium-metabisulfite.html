Product overview

What is food grade Sodium Metabisulfite?

Food grade sodium metabisulfite (SMBS), also called sodium disulfite or sodium pyrosulfite, is a sulfite used in permitted foods for antioxidant, antibrowning, flour-treatment or preservative functions.

For procurement, “food grade” must be supported by a current source-specific specification, suitable manufacturing and quality documents, and a batch COA. Final use depends on the complete formulation, intended function and destination-market rules.

Buyer questions

Food grade Sodium Metabisulfite FAQ

What is food grade Sodium Metabisulfite used for?

Sodium Metabisulfite is used in suitable food processes for sulfite antioxidant, antibrowning agent and preservative functions. Legal use, dosage and performance must be confirmed for the exact food and destination market.

What are the CAS and food additive numbers for Sodium Metabisulfite?

Sodium Metabisulfite is identified here by CAS 7681-57-4 and INS 223. Confirm the identity and supplied form on the current specification.

What specification should a buyer request for Sodium Metabisulfite?

Request the current signed specification, agreed test methods, representative COA and a current batch COA. Resolve every missing unit, comparator or particle-size requirement before approval.

How is food grade Sodium Metabisulfite packed?

25 kg plastic or neutral paper bag. Confirm food-contact liner, palletization, marks and container loading in the quotation.

What should be included in a SMBS RFQ?

Provide the food application, target standard and critical limits, quantity, packing, destination, certificates, Incoterm and requested delivery date.
