Direct product answer

What is food grade Lactic Acid?

Lactic acid is an organic acid used as an acidity regulator in permitted food applications. Commercial food grades can differ in concentration, fermentation or synthetic origin, stereochemical profile, color and heat stability, so the exact grade must be qualified.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact source, intended function, full food process and destination-market rules. This page supports preliminary qualification; the current signed specification and batch COA supplied by sales govern the order.

How to source Lactic Acid in bulk

State the required lactic acid concentration and test basis. Compare delivered acid content, not only the price per kilogram of solution.

INS 270 identity

Use INS 270 alongside the exact chemical form and current specification. The additive number identifies the ingredient; it does not establish every permitted use.

Buyer questions

Lactic Acid FAQ

What does INS 270 mean?

INS 270 identifies lactic acid, an acidity regulator used in permitted food applications. E270 is the corresponding European additive designation. Confirm the exact grade and the rules for the intended finished food.

Is lactic acid the same as sodium lactate?

No. Lactic acid and its sodium salt are different ingredients. They have different composition and formulation roles, so compare them against the required acidity, sodium contribution and finished-food performance.

Why do lactic acid concentrations matter?

Different solution concentrations deliver different amounts of acid per kilogram. Agree the concentration and analytical basis, then compare dilution, dosing, handling and delivered cost.

What should buyers verify about L- and DL-lactic acid?

Confirm the stereochemical profile and matching chemical identity on the current specification. Do not assume an L-only grade from the generic name or CAS listing.

What should a lactic acid RFQ include?

Provide concentration, stereochemical and origin requirements, food application, target pH, process conditions, quantity, packing, destination and documents. Request a current specification and representative COA.
