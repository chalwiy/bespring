Direct product answer

What is food grade Mono- and Diglycerides?

Mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids are food emulsifiers composed of glycerol esters of edible fatty acids. INS 471 covers a family of mixtures, so buyers must qualify mono/di-glyceride balance, fatty-acid profile, source, physical form and application performance.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact source, intended function, full food process and destination-market rules. This page supports preliminary qualification; the current signed specification and batch COA supplied by sales govern the order.

Mono- and Diglycerides FAQ

What is food grade Mono- and Diglycerides?

INS 471 covers a family of mixtures, so buyers must qualify mono/di-glyceride balance, fatty-acid profile, source, physical form and application performance. Which identity should buyers use for Mono- and Diglycerides?

Composition-specific mixture; INS 471; Mono- and diglyceride blend. Where the name covers a family or mixture, confirm the exact member and composition before approval.

Why is there no public Mono- and Diglycerides specification on this page?

Product specifications are source- and contract-specific and are not published on this website. Contact Bespring Chemical sales for the current signed specification or TDS, analytical methods and a representative COA.

What documents are available for Mono- and Diglycerides?

What should a Mono- and Diglycerides RFQ include?
