Direct product answer

What is food grade zinc citrate?

The supplied zinc citrate is identified as dihydrate Zn3(C6H5O7)2·2H2O, CAS 5990-32-9, with reference content of at least 96.0% on a dry basis.

The supplied commercial limits must be matched to the exact grade, intended food category and destination-market rules. A product name alone does not establish regulatory conformity.
