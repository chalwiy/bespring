Direct product answer

What is food grade Carrageenan?

Carrageenan is a sulfated polysaccharide hydrocolloid obtained from red seaweeds. The supplied portfolio includes refined and semi-refined grades with different minimum gel strengths.

Commercial suitability depends on the exact grade, source, test method, application and destination-market rules. The current signed specification and batch COA govern a transaction.
