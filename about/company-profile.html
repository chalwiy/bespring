COMPANY PROFILE

Bespring Chemical Co., Ltd.

Bespring Chemical Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as “Bespring”) traces its origins back to the 1970s. Formerly known as Pizhou No. 2 Chemical Plant, the company was among the earliest manufacturers in China dedicated to the production and distribution of food-grade and industrial-grade phosphates.

With decades of expertise in phosphates, Bespring has built a solid foundation of technical know-how and quality management systems. During the 1980s and 1990s, Bespring continuously optimized its production processes and expanded product applications, gradually establishing a comprehensive phosphate product portfolio for food, feed, and industrial uses.

Entering the 21st century, while maintaining its leading position in the domestic market, the company actively expanded its global presence, exporting products to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Europe, earning wide recognition from customers.

In 2014, the company underwent a major restructuring and was officially renamed Bespring Chemical Co., Ltd., transforming into a modern limited liability company that integrates R&D, production, and sales. With a professional approach and international vision, Bespring has grown into a trusted global supplier of ingredients and raw materials.

As its international business developed, Bespring not only exported its own branded phosphate products but also integrated high-quality resources from its partners, building a diversified and mutually beneficial global supply chain network. After decades of growth, phosphates remain at the heart of Bespring’s business, while the company continues to earn recognition and trust in the global additives and raw materials industry through consistent quality, professional service, and strong reputation.
