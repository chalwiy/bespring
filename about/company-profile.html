Company profile

A China-Based Ingredient and Chemical Supplier

Bespring Chemical Co., Ltd. was established in Jiangsu in 2014. Its catalog covers food ingredients, feed additives and chemical materials for industrial applications.

When requesting a quotation, name the exact material, grade and intended use. Include any source, site or documentation requirements that matter to your approval.

Supplier Qualification Questions

Where can I check Bespring’s product categories?

The product catalog is organized by food, feed, cleaning, water treatment, mining and agricultural applications. Use the grade-specific detail pages when preparing your inquiry.

How can I qualify the production source for my order?

Specify your required legal entity, production or packing site, grade and documentation. Review those details for the proposed supply before approving the order.
