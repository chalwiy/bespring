Identify the Exact Source for the Proposed Grade

Specify any required manufacturing or packing source in your inquiry. Check the proposed source against your approved list and record which site each required document covers.

Site details

What to Confirm About the Production Site

  • Manufacturer or packer legal name and site address.
  • The material, grade and current commercial specification for that source.
  • Which party issues the specification, SDS and lot COA.
  • Requalification or notification requirements if the source changes.

Batch acceptance

Match the Lot to the Agreed Specification

Compare the test criteria and units on the COA with the approved specification. Confirm that the COA and shipment identify the same lot. Keep the site, lot details and packaging marks available for receiving checks.

Order details

Record the Supply Requirements

Include grade, application, specification revision, acceptance tests and packaging requirements. Agree the documents and shipment information needed for order release.

Supply changes

What to Do if the Source Changes

Assess a new site or grade against the original specification. Request any additional lot testing or revised qualification documents your approval process requires before accepting a change.

Buyer questions

Production Source Questions

Can I set a site requirement in my inquiry?

Yes. Include the approved site, legal entity or country restrictions and the related qualification documents you need to review.

Can a sample COA be used for shipment release?

A sample can show the reporting format and methods. For shipment release, check the COA for the lot being delivered and compare it with the agreed specification.
