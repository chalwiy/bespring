Qualify the exact product and source

For a new purchase or a change in supply, review the product specification, shipment test results and any required certificates for the same grade and source. The documents serve different purposes and should all identify the material or site they cover.

What to include in your request

Include the product name, required grade or standard, intended use and destination country. Specify each document you need and any acceptance requirements set by your customer. If the production or packing site is already specified, include it in your inquiry.

Certification requirements for your order

ISO 9001

If your supplier policy requires ISO 9001, specify the legal entity and activity your policy covers. ISO 9001 certification applies to the quality management system within the scope of the certificate.

ISO guidance on certification

Halal and Kosher

Say which certificate you require for the product and grade, and name any certifier specified by your customer. Check that the document covers the exact product and production site for the proposed supply.

Which documents to request

Specification or Technical Data Sheet (TDS)

Review the product identity, grade, acceptance limits and test methods. Confirm the revision before agreeing to a purchase specification.

Safety Data Sheet (SDS)

Check classification, handling and storage information for the exact material and the intended market.

Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Compare the reported results with the agreed specification. For goods receipt, use the COA for the batch supplied.

Check certificate scope before approval

  • Match the certificate holder to the supplier or producer named for your order.
  • Check the activity or products covered and the relevant manufacturing or packing site.
  • Confirm the certificate’s validity with the issuing body.

Questions about lots and production sites

Can I use a sample COA to release a shipment?

Use a sample COA to review the reporting format and test methods. For shipment release, match the lot number on the COA to the material received and compare the results with your agreed specification.

What should I check if the production site changes?

Confirm the new site and request documents for that source. Check whether the exact product and the new site are within each certificate’s scope before approving the change.

Send a document inquiry

For an existing quote or shipment, include your quote reference or lot number so our sales team can identify the supply you are reviewing.
