Procurement FAQ

Questions from International Buyers

Find answers about our product ranges, supply approach, available documents and quotation requirements.

What products does Bespring Chemical supply?

Bespring supplies phosphates, food ingredients, feed additives, water treatment chemicals, mining chemicals, cleaning ingredients and fertilizer raw materials. Browse the product categories for grade-specific information.

How does Bespring source its products?

Bespring combines its own branded phosphate products with selected production partners for a broader chemical ingredient range. Our team can provide the supply-source and product-document information relevant to your order.

What product documents can buyers request?

Request the current specification or technical data sheet, SDS, sample COA and packaging details. Additional quality and regulatory documents depend on the product, grade and supply source.

Does Bespring supply food-grade phosphates?

Yes. Our food ingredient range includes sodium, potassium and calcium phosphates and phosphate blends. Select the specific product grade for your formulation and destination-market requirements.

What information should a quotation request include?

Send the product name, grade or standard, key specifications, intended application, quantity, packaging, destination port, Incoterm and delivery requirements. Include any required quality or regulatory documents.
